About Me

I was born in Teaneck, New Jersey but I was living in East Stroudsburg, Pennsylvania at the time I was born. I lived there for two weeks before I moved to New York City. I lived there for six months before I moved to East Rutherford, New Jersey, where I lived for four years. I got to spend a lot of time in New York City while I was living in East Rutherford. I moved to Coral Springs, Florida for five years, Aiken, South Carolina for five years, and Monroe, Ohio where I've lived for the past 11 years. People ask me if I have a favorite place that I've lived and while I've liked them all for different reasons, New York City will always have a special place in my heart.☺
